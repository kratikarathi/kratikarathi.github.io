● Melady Lab at USC

I have the honor of working with the incredibly smart PhD student Jiacheng Pang developing a decidable procedure for reusing agent skills across environments. 

The repository to this paper is private, since the paper hasn't been published yet.

● UBC Department of Earth, Ocean and Atmospheric Sciences

Worked with the amazing Elly Thistlewaite from UBC EOAS to explore a cost-effective method to analyze geochemical trends in Hawaiian rock samples. Explored several ML models such as Random forest, Gradient Boosting and KNN Clustering with many feature engineering techniques such as PCA and composition data transformation to reduce dimensionality and increase interpretability. Analyzing the geochemistry of these rocks helps shed light on the geochemical composition of the earth's mantle.

The repository to this paper is private, since the paper hasn't been published yet.

● Animal Welfare Program at UBC

One of my best research experiences was working at AWP with the remarkable Shy Sheng and Borbala Foris. We worked together to explore the viability of mixed effects linear model for inter and intra cattle feeding and drinking repeatability and predictability analysis. This analysis helped us in early detection of lameness in cattles, a disease that hinders their ability to walk and disrupts their feeding and drinking habits. We also explored inter-cattle behaviour to determine whether a dominance hierarchical relationship exists and how it effects feed competition.

moo4feed is a R package we developed to extract novel individual-level animal traits from raw feeding and drinking data collected from sensors: https://github.com/skysheng7/moo4feed[moo4feed]

● Non-Invasive Neurostimulation Therapies (NINET) Lab at UBC

My first research experience was working at NINET, cleaning and wrangling individual patient surveys to determine a trend in depression scores over the course of the treatment. This was also my first introduction to REDCap, a web-based software platform used to store surveys for clinical trials and research. I was involved in data ingestion, wrangling and finding ways to deal with corrupt data and missing values
